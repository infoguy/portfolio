Development

How I work

Every project runs through the same four steps

Understand

Who is the learner or user, what do they need to do, and what gets in the way?

Design

Outline the experience, sketch the screens, and get early feedback from SMEs or users.

Build

Develop in the right tool for the job, whether that is Storyline, Figma, or code.

Test and refine

Review for accessibility, clarity, and usability, then iterate.
