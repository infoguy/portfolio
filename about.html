About me

Librarian turned instructional designer turned UX/UI designer

In every one of those jobs the work is the same at its core: understand what someone is trying to do, remove whatever is in the way, and make the path obvious.

I started my career as a librarian, which taught me how people look for information and where they get lost. That question followed me into instructional design, and then into UX.

I have spent seven-plus years in higher education designing, developing, and revising course content and assessments for on-ground, blended, and online programs, with over six years in Canvas. Along the way I added UX research and interface design through the Google UX Design Professional Certificate and front-end development, so I can carry a project from a learning objective or a user problem all the way to a working build.

Today I teach and design at Western Governors University and American Career College, and I build automation tools that give instructional designers their time back.

I live in Frisco, Texas, with my family.
