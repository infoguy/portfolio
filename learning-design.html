Project 1

Dental Terminology Escape Room

A gamified Storyline 360 module that tests dental terminology through an escape room instead of a quiz.

Role: Instructional designer and developerTools: Articulate Storyline 360, Adobe Creative SuiteClient: West Coast UniversityAudience: Dental assisting students

The problem

Dental assisting students had to memorize a large set of terms, and standard quizzes gave them little reason to engage beyond the test.

The approach

I designed the review as an escape room. Each room is a puzzle that can only be solved by applying terminology correctly, so recall becomes the key that moves the learner forward. Immediate feedback replaces a score at the end.

The result

Students practice the full term set in a format they want to finish, with feedback on every puzzle instead of a single score at the end.

Project 2

Reach for the Right Control: Abdominal Imaging Pre-Lab

A Rise 360 microlearning module that teaches sonography students which ultrasound console control to adjust, and why, before they ever touch the equipment.

Role: Instructional designer and developerTools: Articulate Rise 360Client: American Career CollegeAudience: Diagnostic medical sonography students

The problem

Students were arriving at the scanning lab without a plan. Faced with a poor image, they would turn whatever knob was nearest instead of diagnosing the problem first, and limited console time was spent on trial and error rather than practice.

The approach

I built a short pre-lab module around one question: which control, and why. Five controls (gain, TGC, depth, focus, frequency) each get a before-and-after image pair, a one-line reminder of what the control does, when to reach for it, and the effect to expect. Knowledge checks move from single scenarios to a multi-select case to a drag-and-drop matching activity, and the module ends with a Control Log assignment where students justify their choices on four suboptimal images, the same reasoning they will say out loud at the console.

The result

A ten-minute module students complete at home so lab time starts with judgment already in place. Safety practices (ALARA, transducer care, ergonomics, patient tolerance) are folded in rather than taught separately.
